Aligning two texts

Use this tool to experiment with aligning two strings with different parameters. The input can be in IAST/Madras Lexicon transliteration or in Bengali, Burmese, Devanagari, Grantha, Malayalam, Newa, Sarada, Sinhalese, Tamil, Telugu, Tibetan, or Ge'ez scripts. Some options will additionally work for other scripts.
